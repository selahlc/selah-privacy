Privacy Policy for Selah

App: Selah — "Number Your Days"
Package: app.selah.watch
Developer: Selah LC
Contact: selahlc3@gmail.com
Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Selah app for Android and the Selah web app at https://selahlc.com (together, "Selah," "we," "us") handle your information.

Selah is a Christian devotional app. It shows an estimate of the days you may have left and lets you read and listen to the Bible, memorize verses, keep a journal and prayer list, track simple daily habits, and walk with a small circle of invited friends.

Summary

  • You can use Selah without an account. Without one, what you enter stays on your device.
  • If you create an account (optional), your profile, journal, prayers, and progress are saved on our server so they can follow you to other devices and power the friend circle.
  • Journal days you share go only to friends in your circle. Private messages between friends are end-to-end encrypted when both of you have encryption keys set up (see "Messages").
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, no in-app purchases. We don't sell your information.
  • You can delete your account and its data in the app or on the web.

1. Using Selah without an account

You may enter: - your first name and birthday, used to calculate your Count; - how many days a week you plan to exercise and read the Bible, which adjust the estimate and power check-ins and streaks; - journal entries, prayers, kept verses, highlights, bookmarks, reading-plan progress, daily check-ins, and settings; - pictures for journal days, verse backgrounds, or your Count background; - names and invite codes of friends.

Without an account, all of this is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We do not receive it.

2. If you create an account

Signing in is optional. It's needed only for the friend circle and for syncing between devices. You sign in with an email address and password. Sign-in is provided through the authentication service of the Grok app platform that hosts Selah.

When you're signed in, we store:

Information Why
Account details: name, email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash), account ID, sign-in sessions To create and secure your account
Synced profile and progress: name, birthday, weekly exercise and Bible-reading targets, daily check-ins (exercised, read, prayed, assignment), streaks, reading plan and chapters read, saved/liked verses, highlights, bookmarks, kept verses, prayers, journal text, and display settings To keep your data the same on all your devices
Journal days you keep: the text, up to three pictures, and your display name, along with whether the day is shared with your circle To back up your journal and, if you choose, show it to your circle
Circle information: your invite code ("mark"), circle name, the friends you add or remove, likes, privacy choices for each friend, and messages To run the friend circle
Encryption public keys So friends can send you end-to-end encrypted messages

Pictures are resized on your device before upload.

3. The friend circle

The circle is invite-only. You add friends by sharing your code, a link, or a QR code, or by scanning theirs. People in your circle can see your display name and the journal days you choose to share with them, including their pictures, and can like them and message you. The circle shows shared days from the past week. A journal day is shared only when you check "Share with the friend circle." Otherwise it's saved to your account but not shown to friends. You can remove a friend ("Let go") at any time.

4. Messages

When you message a friend, Selah tries to encrypt the message on your device (using P-256 key agreement and AES-GCM). It can then be read only on your friend's device, not by us. If encryption can't be set up (for example, if your friend hasn't published encryption keys yet), the message is sent without end-to-end encryption. It is still protected in transit by HTTPS, but it's stored on our server in a form we could read. Encryption private keys stay on your device.

5. Camera and photos

Selah uses the camera and lets you choose pictures only when you: - add a picture to a journal day, set a picture behind a verse, or set a picture behind your Count; or - scan a friend's circle QR code.

QR scanning happens on your device, and camera frames are not saved or sent. Verse and Count background pictures stay on your device. Journal pictures are uploaded with the journal day if you're signed in (see section 2). Camera and photo access are optional and can be turned off in Android Settings.

6. Content the app downloads

To show some content, Selah makes requests over HTTPS to: - audio.wordproject.org, for recorded Bible chapters; - bible-api.com, as a backup source of Bible text; - Google Fonts, for typefaces.

These requests name only the book and chapter, or the font, and don't include your account or journal. Like any internet request, they show your IP address and device/browser details to that service.

7. Where your data is stored and who processes it

Selah's server runs at selahlc.com on the Grok app-hosting platform. That platform uses cloud infrastructure providers, including Vercel and Cloudflare, which process data on our behalf to host the service. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States. These providers handle data under their own terms. Like most websites, they may keep technical logs such as IP addresses and request times.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We don't use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools. We may disclose information if required by law.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data, synced data, journal days, circle data, and messages are kept until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Information on your device stays until you delete it, use Recast, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Copies held by our hosting providers in backups or logs are removed according to their retention schedules.

9. Deleting your data

  • In the app: while signed in, open the Circle tab and tap Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored with it on our server: sign-in, synced data, journal days and pictures, circle membership, shared days, likes, messages, and keys. It also clears the Selah data on that device.
  • On the web: go to https://selahlc.com/delete (sign in, then tap "Delete my account"), or email selahlc3@gmail.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete your account. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.
  • You can also delete individual journal entries, pictures, prayers, and friends at any time.

Journal days you shared, or messages you sent, may already have been seen by friends before you deleted them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. The app's "Carry a copy" feature exports your data to a file you control. For any other request, email selahlc3@gmail.com.

11. Security

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and the app blocks unencrypted traffic. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. Private circle messages are end-to-end encrypted when possible (section 4). Journal text and pictures are not end-to-end encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

12. Children

Selah is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

13. Changes

We'll update this policy and its effective date when our practices change, and we'll tell you in the app if a change is significant.
